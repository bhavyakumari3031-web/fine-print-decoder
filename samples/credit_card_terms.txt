ZenCard Credit Card - Terms
1. Joining fee: Rs 999. Annual fee: Rs 1,499 from year 2, waived only if you spend Rs 3,00,000 in a year.
2. Interest: 3.9% per month (46.8% per year). There is no interest-free period if you pay less than the full amount.
3. Minimum due is 5% of the balance. Paying only the minimum keeps the debt growing.
4. Cash withdrawal: 2.5% fee (minimum Rs 500), with interest from day one.
5. Late payment fee: Rs 1,300.
6. Reward points expire after 12 months. Redeeming costs Rs 99.
7. Foreign transaction markup: 3.5%.
8. The bank can cancel the card or cut your limit at any time without notice.
