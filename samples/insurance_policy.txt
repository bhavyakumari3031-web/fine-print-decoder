SafeLife Health Insurance - Policy Terms
1. Sum insured: Rs 5,00,000.
2. Pre-existing diseases are not covered for 4 years. Claims in the first 30 days are not covered, except accidents.
3. Room rent is capped at 1% of the sum insured per day. If you take a costlier room, all other bills are cut in the same proportion.
4. Co-payment: you pay 20% of every claim.
5. Exclusions: dental, spectacles, and any condition the insurer considers "lifestyle related".
6. You must inform the insurer within 24 hours of admission, or the claim may be rejected.
7. Premium may rise by up to 25% at each renewal.
